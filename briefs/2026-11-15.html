Straits Times and Mothership

Fullerton Health breach exposes 140,000 migrant worker records

A standalone server behind Fullerton Health's primary care plan for migrant workers was accessed without authorisation, exposing names, ID numbers, birth dates and addresses. No medical or financial data was taken. The company has isolated the server and is building a new one, and MOM says workers should watch for scams.

Straits Times

Real police calls now come from one number: 6000 0999

Since 2 November, every outgoing call from the Singapore Police Force to the public uses the single number 6000 0999, so people can tell genuine officers from impersonators. Scams posing as government officials cost victims at least S$142.5 million across more than 3,200 cases between January and September this year.

Straits Times

iMessage scams posing as couriers and polyclinics take S$31,000

Police say at least S$31,000 has been lost since 31 October to Apple iMessage texts pretending to be courier firms or SingHealth polyclinics. The messages claim failed deliveries or unpaid bills and link to fake sites that harvest bank and card details. Police advise checking links with ScamShield and keeping devices updated.

Straits Times

PM Wong in Manila as ASEAN signs its first regional digital economy pact

Lawrence Wong is in Manila for the 49th ASEAN Summit, which runs from 15 to 17 November. Ministers signed the ASEAN Digital Economy Framework Agreement on 14 November after three years of talks, setting shared rules on digital trade, data flows and e-payments that aim to double the region's digital economy by 2030.

Straits Times

Family stranded at Changi after Trip.com booking vanishes

A Singapore family arrived at Changi for a China Southern flight to Chengdu booked through Trip.com, only to find the airline had no record of it. They paid an extra S$1,805 for a 3am flight, then learned their return tickets were also invalid. Trip.com says it is still checking with the airline.

Closed brake valve likely caused Kolkata-Sairang Express derailment

A Northeast Frontier Railway inquiry found a brake-pipe valve on the locomotive was in the closed position, so the brakes may not have worked when the driver applied them before the 11 November derailment at Digaru in Assam. The Railway Board has ordered brake tests on locomotives nationwide until 18 November.

Trump, Putin and Xi plan a joint meeting in China next week

The White House says the three leaders will meet on the sidelines of the Apec summit in Shenzhen, with Trump wanting to discuss cutting nuclear arsenals. Iran may come up, but Ukraine is not expected to be on the agenda. It would be the first such meeting of the three powers' leaders.

Zelensky says the US floated Donbas for Nato membership

President Zelensky says US negotiators raised the idea of Ukraine pulling its remaining troops out of the eastern Donbas in exchange for joining Nato. Washington has not confirmed it, with an official saying many ideas are being discussed and any final deal must be agreed by both sides.

BBC News

Italy fines Meta 805 million euros over Facebook photo access

Italy's data protection authority says Facebook's Android app reached into about 20 million users' photo galleries without specific consent, through a feature suggesting pictures to post. Meta has 60 days to switch the feature off by default and ask permission separately. It is among the largest fines Italy has imposed on a tech firm.

BBC News

More than 100,000 join Yellow Vest cost-of-living protests in France

The interior ministry counted 106,000 protesters nationwide, 24,000 of them in Paris, down from 146,000 a week earlier. The marches, organised by the Yellow Vests and youth groups, targeted living costs, the Macron government, police violence and school conditions, and stayed mostly calm despite some tear gas.
