9to5Mac

Apple Intelligence gets China's approval for iPad and Mac

Shanghai's cyberspace regulator says Apple Intelligence is now registered for tablets and laptops, a required step for generative AI services in China. iPhones were cleared in July, and Apple plans a December launch there with iOS 27.3, using Alibaba's Qwen model, but has not said whether iPad and Mac support arrives at the same time.

9to5Mac

Apple starts charging for extra AI use, beginning with Logic Pro

The latest Logic Pro update offers a one-time US$49.99 'intelligence usage pack' for people who run past the included allowance of AI features. It is the first time Apple has charged for Apple Intelligence, and similar packs are expected in Final Cut Pro, Pixelmator Pro and iWork when the other Creator Studio updates arrive this month.

MacRumors

iOS 27.2.1 and macOS 27.2.1 close Messages and AirPlay holes

Monday's updates fix a bug that could let a remote attacker join an iMessage group chat, plus several AirPlay and Bluetooth flaws, including one letting a malicious device pair without user action. The Mac update also finishes the eMudhra certificate fix for command-line tools. Apple has not reported any of these being exploited, but suggests updating promptly.

MacRumors

Singapore cut Apple's tax bill by US$2.67 billion this year

Apple's first country-by-country tax disclosure shows it paid US$4.96 billion in Ireland and US$3.25 billion in Singapore in fiscal 2026. Measured against the 21 per cent US rate, Singapore saved it slightly more than Ireland did, even after a new US$2.51 billion top-up tax that lifts big multinationals to a 15 per cent minimum.

Straits Times and Mothership

About 100 evacuated after a basement fire at Orchard Gateway

An electrical equipment room two floors below the Orchard Road mall caught fire at about 5.40pm on Monday, and police cleared roughly 100 people as a precaution. SCDF put it out with foam and extinguishers, nobody was hurt, and the cause is being investigated. It follows a fire at Kinokuniya in Ngee Ann City a week earlier.

Straits Times

Hidden wartime fuel tunnels in Marsiling will be properly studied

SLA, the National Heritage Board and URA say the muddy tunnels in a 12.5ha forest hold the only known pre-war or wartime underground fuel tank left in Singapore and the region. A consultant will carry out heritage, archaeological, structural and unexploded-ordnance surveys to guide what happens to the site next.

Straits Times

Three in four parents say primary school tuition is a must

A survey of about 4,000 people for advocacy group EveryChild.SG found families spend a median S$600 a month per child on tuition. Most parents want less academic pressure but fear their children will fall behind at PSLE, and 77 per cent would ease off if an optional-PSLE system guaranteed a secondary school place.

Straits Times

Four men accused of using a stranger's home to chase S$6 million in loans

Police say the men, aged 27 to 39, impersonated a homeowner with a forged NRIC, lured him out twice so valuers could inspect his property, and applied to two credit companies for more than S$6 million. The owner raised the alarm in April after finding a caveat on his home. They are due in court today.

Trump calls the Iran war 'effectively over', but the blockade stays

After meeting Qatar's prime minister, Trump said Iran was finished militarily and the US would keep the status quo at the request of Gulf neighbours, meaning the naval blockade and a new effort to cut land and Caspian trade continue. France blames Iran's Revolutionary Guards for hacking supermarket fridges

Foreign minister Jean-Noël Barrot said an IRGC-linked cyber unit took over digital thermostats in October, pushing temperatures above 50C and forcing shops to throw out stock. Models and products

Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

Anthropic says the new models are better at coding, reasoning and vision and respond faster than the 5.1 versions. Fable 5.2 is available now and aimed at hard engineering work and long-running agent projects, while Mythos 5.2 goes only to vetted organisations. The company says testing included solving two open black-hole physics problems.

The Verge

Meta's Muse Ember is a cheap model built for agent grunt work

Meta Superintelligence Lab's lightest model costs 10 US cents per million input tokens and is meant for repetitive agent tasks such as tool calls, routing and document parsing. Meta pitches it as a partner to the larger Muse Wonder, which plans the work, and says it beats Anthropic's Haiku and OpenAI's Luna on cost per task.

TechCrunch

DeepSeek's Harness agent tool passes 1.5 million daily users

The Hangzhou start-up says its open-source agent framework reached 1.54 million daily active users less than three months after launch, up from about 271,000 at the end of September. API users were burning through roughly 92 trillion tokens a day, and DeepSeek plans a plug-in marketplace to take on OpenAI and Anthropic.

South China Morning Post

Disney and Adobe will build custom AI models on Disney's characters

Announced at Adobe MAX, the multiyear deal uses Adobe's Firefly Foundry to train proprietary models on Disney's characters, settings and visual styles, aimed at commercially safe output. Disney gets early access to Adobe's AI tools for its studios, theme parks, cruise line and ESPN graphics, and Imagineering already uses Firefly.

Money

Anthropic spends US$3.4 billion on gas turbines to power its AI

According to the Financial Times, Anthropic is buying Caterpillar generators that can produce up to 1.7 gigawatts, originally ordered by equipment supplier OnePWR. It marks a shift for a company that has mostly rented computing power, as turbine backlogs stretch into the 2030s and grid connections take years.

TechCrunch

Mitsui Chemicals agrees to buy chipmaking materials firm JSR

The roughly US$12 billion deal, including debt, buys JSR from state-backed Japan Investment Corp and is the biggest in Japan's chemicals industry. JSR's photoresists, used to print circuits on wafers, go to TSMC, Intel and Samsung, and Mitsui is betting on demand from AI chips. Closing is expected in fiscal 2027.

The California senator behind the vetoed SB 1047 and the signed SB 53 won Nancy Pelosi's old San Francisco seat. Tsinghua researcher says China's AI future lies in industrial systems

Cui Peng, an associate professor at Tsinghua University, argues that Chinese AI is moving beyond general chatbots towards specialised systems that read factory sensor data, financial records and other industrial information. He told SCMP that such specialist tools, rather than ever-bigger language models, could become a crucial part of the next phase of AI.
