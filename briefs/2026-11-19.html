Category A COE drops 6.4 per cent but still costs more than Category B

Premiums for smaller cars fell to S$130,100 from a record S$139,001, and Category B slid 5.1 per cent to S$125,600. Only the Open category edged up, to S$136,000. Dealers point to weaker showroom traffic and tighter loan rules for private-hire buyers, and expect prices to climb again later.

ERP gantries start coming down ahead of satellite road charging on 1 January

LTA says most gantries will be removed or modified by June 2027. Drivers will instead see blue oval 'ERP' markings on the road, two on arterial roads and three on expressways, plus 'ERP Charge Zone' signs, while the on-board unit warns of each charge point, the rate and the amount deducted.

Trump, Xi and Putin meet together for the first time in Shenzhen

Xi greeted Trump on the tarmac for the Apec summit, and Trump later shook hands with Putin, their first meeting in over a year. The three leaders hold talks on Thursday, with Ukraine, Iran, nuclear weapons, trade and AI all expected on the table. Trump and Putin also meet one-on-one.

The International Court of Justice rules on Thursday in the case The Gambia filed seven years ago over the army's 2017 expulsion of the Rohingya from Rakhine State. Individuals have been convicted of genocide before, but no state has ever been found guilty of it by an international court.

Troops advanced east of Nuseirat refugee camp after evacuation orders, in what Israel calls a targeted raid on a tunnel in the part of Gaza it does not control. Hamas called it a flagrant breach of the October 2025 ceasefire. Israel holds more than 60 per cent of the territory.
