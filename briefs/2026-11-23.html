9to5Mac

iOS 27's Connectivity Assist leans on cellular when Wi-Fi turns weak

A new iOS 27 setting called Connectivity Assist notices when a Wi-Fi network is slow or barely usable, such as at the edge of your driveway or in a busy café, and quietly tops up with cellular data. It can be switched off per network and stays off while roaming or in Low Data Mode.

9to5Mac

Apple tried a two-widget column on the iPhone Duo Lock Screen

Code spotted by the leaker pdfu shows Apple tested a 'complication column' along the right edge of the iPhone Duo's Lock Screen, holding two small widgets between the status icons and the camera and torch buttons. It first appeared in iOS 27.1 and has not changed since, so it may have been shelved.

Gurman: Siri job cuts hit the old system, not the new AI work

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman says Apple's recent Siri layoffs fell on staff maintaining the assistant's older infrastructure. PM Wong flies into Western Sydney's new airport to start an Australia visit

Lawrence Wong lands today on Singapore Airlines' first flight to Western Sydney International, becoming the first foreign leader to arrive there. In Canberra on 24 November he and Anthony Albanese co-chair the 11th annual leaders' meeting, with supply resilience for food and energy high on the agenda, before he heads to Sydney.

Straits Times

Wong says chips re-exported through Singapore are a 'real concern'

Wrapping up his China trip, the Prime Minister welcomed genuine Chinese investment but said using Singapore to dodge other countries' export controls is a problem. Chips booked through Singapore and shipped elsewhere far outnumber those used locally, he said, and Singapore can share information with the US but cannot enforce on its behalf abroad.

Mothership

Strokes are rising, and fastest among younger adults

Yearly stroke cases climbed from 6,720 to 10,664 between 2013 and 2023, mostly because Singapore is ageing, but age-adjusted rates rose too, by about 8 per cent. Doctors blame salty, processed diets and inactivity, which are pushing up blood pressure, diabetes and obesity in people in their twenties and thirties.

Straits Times

Arrests of girls and young women reach a five-year high

MSF says 792 females aged 10 to 20 were arrested in 2025, up about 20 per cent from 662 a year earlier. Cheating led the list, often girls lending bank accounts, Singpass logins or SIM cards to scammers, followed by theft. Officials say friends and boyfriends often draw them into offending.

Straits Times

YMCA says a breach may have exposed data on 13,000 people

Its Youth For Causes project portal was broken into in October and taken offline on 19 October. Names, schools, full or partial NRIC numbers and contacts of participants, next of kin, teachers and judges were held there. Investigators could not confirm whether data was copied, so YMCA is contacting everyone affected.

Indian Express

Manipur hooch deaths reach 17 as the state orders an inquiry

Seventeen people have died in five days after drinking suspected spurious liquor in Chandel and nearby districts. A committee led by a principal secretary must report within a month. More than 1,100 people have been screened, 228 are in hospital, 38 on dialysis, and over 4,500 litres of illicit liquor have been seized.

Indian Express

Kaiga's first reactor restarts after a 600-day overhaul

Unit 1 at the Kaiga plant in Karnataka reached criticality again early Sunday after replacing its coolant channels and feeders, giving the 25-year-old reactor a second life. NPCIL says no unit of its fleet has come back faster. Two 700 MW units under construction will take the site to 2,280 MW.

Five stories from the day

COP31 ends in Turkey without a main climate deal

After running into Sunday, the longest UN climate summit ever closed without agreement. The EU and Pacific islands rejected a text that never named fossil fuels, and poorer countries objected to the lack of firm adaptation money. It is the first collapse of a COP's main deal since Copenhagen in 2009.

BBC News

Democrats reach 258 House seats, their best result since 1992

Chuck Hubbard beat long-serving Republican Virginia Foxx in North Carolina by 330 votes after a recount, giving Democrats at least 258 seats to the Republicans' 173, with four races still open. The party also won the Senate in the midterms.

BBC News

Suspected drone attack sets a Russian ship ablaze in Benghazi

An explosion hit a Russian vessel at Benghazi's naval base at dawn on Sunday while it refuelled on the way home, Libya's eastern government said, reporting no casualties. A commercial ship was also targeted off the coast later in the day, leaving minor injuries. Moscow has not commented.

BBC News

South Korea's education minister quits over AI-assisted exam cheating

Choi Kyo-jin resigned after a top scorer in a national accountancy exam admitted using a hidden camera and phone, days before the university entrance exam. The rushed response included Wi-Fi blocking, doubled supervisors, pencil-only rules and a reward of at least US$75,000 for tip-offs, but teachers called it belated.

Negotiators for caretaker PM Benjamin Netanyahu's Likud and Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar met in Herzliya on Sunday but stayed split over who would lead the next government and whether far-right minister Itamar Ben-Gvir would keep a seat at the table, leaving Israel's post-election coalition picture open.
