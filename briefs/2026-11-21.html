iOS 27 lets two iPhones share one phone number

A feature called iPhone Handoff makes one phone the main device and a second one a shared secondary. Whichever iPhone you unlock takes over the number, with a 'Switched to this iPhone' banner to confirm. Only one is active at a time, and some carrier services such as call forwarding stay on the main phone.

Signal now blocks screenshots on iPhone to keep Siri AI out of chats

A new 'Block Siri AI' setting, switched on by default, stops screenshots inside Signal and in the app switcher. Signal told Reuters that Apple does not yet give developers better ways to shield app content from Siri AI and other agents. Users who want screenshots back can turn the toggle off in privacy settings.

Singapore

Straits Times and Mothership

Singapore and China start talks to upgrade their free trade agreement

Prime Minister Lawrence Wong announced the negotiations after meeting Xi Jinping in Shenzhen following the APEC summit. The upgrade will cover new areas such as the digital economy and cross-border data flows. The two leaders also discussed working together on emerging technologies, especially artificial intelligence, to help businesses and train workers.

Mothership

782,000 older Singaporeans will get a CPF top-up of up to S$1,500 by 8 December

The S$1.04 billion payout, announced at Budget 2026, goes to citizens born in 1976 or earlier who live here, have CPF savings below the S$110,200 Basic Retirement Sum and a home with an annual value of no more than S$31,000. It is credited automatically, and the amount is tiered by need.

Straits Times

Work starts on the largest phase of Tuas Port

Construction has begun on the third of four sections, built on nearly 400 hectares of reclaimed land. It will open in 2028 and reach 21 deepwater berths by the mid-2030s. Once finished in the 2040s, Tuas will hold all of Singapore's container operations and become the world's largest fully automated port.

Straits Times

More national servicemen will lead as cohorts shrink, says Chan Chun Sing

The defence minister said the SAF is moving to smaller units with more technology, so a bigger share of each NSF cohort will take on command roles even as birth rates cut intake. New infantry battalions will have five platoons per rifle company and carry four types of drones.

Straits Times

Sentosa cable car rides are free every morning until 20 December

From 21 November, anyone can board the Sentosa Line without a ticket between 8.45am and 11am daily, at Sensoryscape, Imbiah Lookout or Siloso Point. The stations link to Resorts World, the luge and Siloso Beach. The Mount Faber Line from HarbourFront remains a paid service during the promotion.

Congress beats the BJP in Rajasthan's rural local elections

Declared results give Congress 2,990 panchayat samiti seats to the BJP's 1,795, and 143 zila parishad seats to 83, reversing the 2020-21 rural polls. Hanuman Beniwal's RLP won 438 samiti seats. It comes months after the BJP took 197 of the state's 301 urban local bodies.

Core sector output grows 10.9 per cent in October, a series high

Electricity, coal, cement and iron ore drove the jump, up from 8.2 per cent in September. Economists note that October 2025 grew only 0.8 per cent because of the festive calendar, so much of the gain is a base effect. ICRA's Aditi Nayar called the figure largely optical.

The court acted on a petition from a 20-year-old law student at NLSIU, born to first cousins, who lives with genetic eye disorders. Five stories from the day

Russian cruise missile crashes in Poland during a huge attack on Ukraine

Poland says a Kh-101 missile was one of three objects that entered its airspace early on Friday, hitting farmland near Nowy Lipowiec about 50km from the Ukrainian border. Nobody was hurt. Prime Minister Donald Tusk said it did not look accidental, coming while Western allies held military exercises nearby. Russia has not commented.

UK and EU agree a deal on food checks, carbon trading and youth visas

Prime Minister Andy Burnham signed the latest stage of the post-Brexit reset in Brussels, dropping most checks on British food exports in return for following some EU rules. The Treasury says it could add up to £17bn a year. Burnham wants still closer ties; the Conservatives and Reform called it unfair and a betrayal.

Danish intelligence says Russian services planned and set in motion an attempt to kill the performer known as Kyivstoner at a concert near Copenhagen in September, and that it stopped the attackers. Two people have been charged and deny it. Russia's embassy calls the claims unsubstantiated and promises a response.

BBC News

COP31 runs into overtime after fossil fuels vanish from the draft deal

Talks in Antalya, Turkiye, were due to end on Friday, but nearly 200 countries remain split. Australia's opening draft called for a faster move away from fossil fuels; Thursday's version dropped every mention after Saudi Arabia and other producers objected. Environmental groups warn it could be the worst COP ever. A new draft is expected Saturday.
