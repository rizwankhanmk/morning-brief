9to5Mac

Binance warns iPhone users about an iOS flaw researchers say is still unpatched

Crypto exchange Binance says a flaw researchers call worse_query lets a malicious app read files stored by other apps, putting seed phrases, private keys and passwords at risk. The researchers who published a proof of concept say neither iOS 27.2.1 nor the first iOS 27.3 beta fixes it, and Apple has not mentioned a patch.

Thailand meets Apple after politicians receive spyware warnings

Thailand's digital economy minister met Apple representatives after Thai politicians received Apple's 29 October alerts about mercenary spyware, Bloomberg reports. He said an alert does not prove a phone was hacked and there is no evidence yet that Pegasus was used. Thai officials plan to meet other tech companies on Thursday.

Google's Gemini app starts rolling out on CarPlay

Google is rolling out a dedicated Gemini app for CarPlay worldwide in several languages, so drivers can talk to the assistant from the dashboard. Conversations sync with the iPhone app, and Gemini can draw on Gmail and Google Calendar for a spoken daily briefing. Drivers need the latest version of the Gemini app on their iPhone.

Straits Times and Mothership

Minmed is the third healthcare provider hit by a cyber attack in a week

Healthcare group Minmed says a system behind its teleconsultation and health screening services was hit by a malicious attack, following breaches at Fullerton Health and Doctor Anywhere. Separately, Dementia Singapore said names, email addresses and phone numbers of some users of its CARA app may have been exposed.

Straits Times

HDB's yearly deficit shrinks to S$4.7 billion

The Housing Board's net deficit for the year to March 2026 fell to S$4.7 billion from S$6.34 billion, driven mostly by expected losses on flats being built and sold below cost. It completed 15,417 flats, fewer than the year before, but had more than 100,000 under construction and handed keys to 19,254 households.

Straits Times

Higher income ceilings bring the busiest BTO launch since 2022

With new income ceilings of S$16,000 for families and S$8,000 for singles, November's BTO launch drew 35,603 applicants for 7,964 flats, or 4.5 per flat, the highest rate since August 2022. Four-room flats in Toa Payoh and Tengah were the most sought after by first-time buyers.

Straits Times

MOH proposes clearer consent rules for young people seeking mental health care

The Ministry of Health has proposed guidelines that tie parental consent rules to the type of service, and would let young people get some care without a parent's approval when that is in their best interest. About one in three Singaporeans aged 10 to 18 report symptoms, and most do not seek help. Feedback closes on 8 December.

Straits Times

Joo Chiat gets a Shibuya-style scramble crossing for three months

From 16 November, the junction of Joo Chiat Road and East Coast Road outside i12 Katong becomes a scramble crossing on trial. Pedestrians can cross diagonally while all traffic stops, the right turn from East Coast Road into Joo Chiat Road ends for good, and LTA warns that waits may be longer.

Five stories from the day

Navi Mumbai tops the league of past winners in India's cleanliness awards

Visakhapatnam was named the cleanest city with over a million people in the Swachh Survekshan awards, ahead of Jabalpur and Lucknow. Navi Mumbai topped the Super Swachh League for previous winners, followed by Surat and Vijayawada. New categories named Puri the cleanest coastal town and Rewa the cleanest riverfront town.

Indian Express

India trims its visa categories from 13 to nine

The Home Ministry has cut the main visa categories from 13 to nine and sub-categories from 49 to 23, and removed 65 to 70 per cent of the questions on application forms. Visas under the new framework will be issued from the end of the year, and the old tourist visa becomes a Visitor Visa.

Indian Express

Supreme Court says a foreign divorce cannot end an Indian marriage on unrecognised grounds

The Supreme Court ruled that a divorce granted abroad on grounds Indian law does not accept, such as irretrievable breakdown, does not dissolve a marriage solemnised in India. A man who remarried after such a decree can face bigamy charges, even if he has since become a citizen of another country.

Times of India

Canada expects a trade deal in time for Modi's December visit

Canada's high commissioner Christopher Cooter says Ottawa is confident of concluding a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement before Narendra Modi visits Canada on 11 and 12 December, with a sixth round of talks this week. Ratification will take longer because provinces are involved, and agreements on business travel and space may follow.

Times of India

Renewables saved India over US$100 billion in fuel imports, IEA says

An International Energy Agency report says solar, wind and bioenergy built over 15 years spared India more than US$100 billion in coal and gas imports. With about 300 GW installed and 530 GW expected by 2030, cumulative savings could reach US$240 billion by then, with yearly savings near US$35 billion.

Five stories from the day

Millions join Spain's first general strike in 14 years over housing

Tenants' groups called the 24-hour stoppage over soaring rents and house prices, and unions joined in. Participation reached 90 per cent in car plants and ports, police clashed with protesters in Barcelona, and the strike lands weeks before an election in which the far right could win power.

The Guardian

Democrat Scott Colom wins Mississippi's Senate seat in an upset

Colom narrowly beat Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith in a state Trump won by 22 points, after a count that took more than a week. He is the first Black senator elected from Mississippi in over 140 years, and Democrats now hold 55 Senate seats with Alaska still to be called.

BBC News

Indonesia's vice-president submits his resignation

Gibran Rakabuming Raka has handed his resignation to President Prabowo Subianto, an aide says, after the Supreme Court ruled a school document from his 2024 campaign invalid. More than 100 lawmakers had backed a move that could have led to impeachment. It is not yet clear whether Prabowo has accepted it.

BBC News

Hoax bomb threats hit thousands of schools in Italy and the UK

About 6,300 schools across Italy and more than 700 in Scotland and Northern Ireland received emailed bomb threats on Wednesday. Some buildings were evacuated, but authorities found no sign of real danger, and Giorgia Meloni called it an attempt to cause alarm. Cybercrime officers are tracing the source.

BBC News

Saudi oil output climbs back near pre-war levels, OPEC says

Saudi Arabia pumped 10.56 million barrels a day in October, its most since the Iran war began in February, according to OPEC. Output across OPEC+ rose by about two million barrels a day to 41.23 million, helped by Russia after Washington lifted sanctions on its energy sector.

DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 links DNA changes to specific cells

Google DeepMind's new model predicts how genetic variants change gene activity in particular cell types and states, after training on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues.
