Straits Times and Mothership

Singapore serves legal notices on 10 local firms over haze links

NEA has asked ten Singapore-registered palm oil and forestry companies for details of their Indonesian land and fire-prevention steps, and issued preventive notices to ten Indonesian firms. Minister Grace Fu says errant companies will be held to account where the evidence supports it.

Mothership

Grace Fu says tap water is safe, but skip haze rainwater

After residents collected blackish rainwater, the minister said reservoir water is treated to remove particles and PUB found nothing unusual. Rain caught during haze can be slightly more acidic and carry more particles, so she advised against drinking, cooking or washing with it.

Mothership

Indonesia trims natural gas exports to Singapore from 24 October

Indonesia's upstream regulator says gas shipments will fall by 31 billion British thermal units a day, hitting only Singapore because of its direct pipelines. The cut follows a minister's warning about curbs on countries that disrespect Indonesia, though the regulator says the reduction was planned for years.

Mothership

Singapore to legislate in early 2027 to protect young social media users

The planned law would require effective age checks and safer-by-design features such as autoplay limits and daily time caps, plus curbs on harmful content and unwanted contact. Consultation found parents and young people prefer graduated access over an outright ban.
