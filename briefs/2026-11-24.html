Straits Times and Mothership

Singapore and China sign 29 deals, a recent high, at their top forum

DPM Gan Kim Yong and Vice-Premier Ding Xuexiang oversaw agreements on trade, finance, science and public health at the 22nd Joint Council for Bilateral Cooperation, up from 27 last year. Gan said upgrading the 2009 free trade agreement, with a focus on digital trade and data flows, could take a year or two.

Straits Times

Owner-occupiers get a one-off property tax rebate in 2027

HDB owner-occupiers get 15 per cent off their 2027 property tax and private home owners 10 per cent, capped at $100, down from a $500 cap this year. One- and two-room flat owners still pay nothing, larger HDB flats will pay about $2 to $4 more a month, and social support thresholds rise from 1 March.

Straits Times

HDB plans a new Pasir Ris estate but will keep 13ha of nature

A forested plot at Pasir Ris Drive 4, zoned for housing since 1980, will become a mix of public and private homes over the next decade. After an environmental study, HDB proposes keeping 8ha of mangrove, seagrass and intertidal habitat plus a 5ha green buffer, and says no brownfield site could match its capacity.

Straits Times

PUB warns of flash flood risk during high tides from Thursday

Tides of 3.3m are expected from 26 to 28 November, roughly 10.30am to 1pm, with tides of up to 3.5m around Christmas and in late January. Low-lying coastal spots such as East Coast Park and Pulau Ubin can flood even without rain, and heavy monsoon downpours at the same time would make it worse.

India offers Nepal a Rs 15,000 crore package after August floods

Hosting Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah in Delhi, Narendra Modi announced the package, about US$1.6 billion, for flood reconstruction and long-term development. The two launched a weekly Ayodhya-Janakpur train and advanced hydropower and pipeline projects, and India promised an IIT offshore campus and a start-up incubator in Nepal.

Indian Express

Pakistani operatives found lurking in defence WhatsApp groups

The Border Roads Organisation has warned that Pakistan-based intelligence operatives got into 11 unofficial WhatsApp groups used by defence, paramilitary and civil defence personnel. Using spoofed or compromised Indian numbers, they stayed silent and watched what members shared, in what the advisory calls a targeted cyber-espionage campaign.

The Hindu

Centre tells people to keep sensitive work off WhatsApp Web

An advisory from the Home Ministry's Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre says a live WhatsApp Web session can let attackers read messages, impersonate users and run phishing scams. Closing the tab does not log you out, it warns, so users should sign out manually and avoid shared, office or cyber cafe computers.

Indian Express

No Winter Session dates as Parliament stays unprorogued past 100 days

The Monsoon Session has now gone more than 100 days without being prorogued, a record, and no Winter Session has been called. That session has been skipped only four times: during the Emergency, a dissolved Lok Sabha, Indira Gandhi's assassination and Covid. Talk is growing of a short special session on delimitation.

Saurav Das, the party's co-convener, says immigration officers at Delhi airport stopped him before his 1am flight to London, where he was due to speak at the University of Oxford about its youth movement. He blamed the Modi government but has not said what reason officials gave him.

Iran flogs singer Parastoo Ahmadi for performing without a hijab

Ahmadi, 29, and four men from her band and production team were each given 74 lashes in Qom, their lawyers say, over a livestreamed 2024 concert in which she wore a sleeveless dress. A court had convicted them of offending public decency and banned them from performing or travelling for two years.

Moscow summoned Estonia's chargé d'affaires after Tallinn said it would close their border, and accused it of letting Ukrainian drones use its airspace, obstructing Russian shipping and banning grain transit. Nigeria boat carrying funeral mourners capsizes, 29 bodies found

Rescuers have pulled 50 people alive from the water after the overcrowded boat sank on Friday on its way from Ayama. Police say it developed engine trouble and the operator ignored passengers' pleas to head for land. The government has ordered stricter safety enforcement on inland waterways.
