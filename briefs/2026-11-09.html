Each yearly software cycle normally runs in three stages of four weeks of coding and four weeks of integration. Straits Times and Mothership

This year's haze has cost Singapore an estimated S$2.32 billion

A preliminary estimate by NTU's Euston Quah and SUSS's Eugene Or puts the economic cost of the 2026 haze, the worst in over a decade, at S$2.32 billion as at 31 October. That is about 4 per cent more than 2015 in today's prices, despite fewer hazy days, and the authors argue it strengthens the case for funding prevention.

Straits Times

IMH's new name, National Centre for Mental Health, comes with a bigger job

The Institute of Mental Health becomes the National Centre for Mental Health in April 2027. Chief executive Jamie Mervyn Lim says it is not cosmetic: the hospital will coordinate a tiered system linking GPs, polyclinics, social agencies and hospitals, and expand in phases to 2040 while focusing on the most complex cases.

Straits Times

Grace Fu says climate talks at COP31 matter more, not less

Ahead of the UN climate conference, the sustainability minister said moving away from fossil fuels will be as hard as before or harder, with energy security biting and rich countries spending more on defence. She said it is still important to keep world leaders' attention on climate action despite these competing priorities.

Straits Times

Biscuit King, an old-school snack shop on Casuarina Road, will close in January

One of the last shops in Singapore selling traditional biscuits and sweets will shut on 31 January 2027. It thanked customers for years of loyalty but gave no reason, though its website says orders are paused because of a manpower shortage. The news drew a wave of nostalgia online.
