Rena Lee becomes the first Singaporean elected as a judge of the International Court of Justice

Singapore's Rena Lee has won a seat on the world court in The Hague, the first Singaporean to do so. She is also only the second candidate from South-east Asia elected to the ICJ since it was founded in 1945.

Straits Times

Missed security update behind 9.5-hour public healthcare outage

The health ministry has apologised in Parliament for a preventable lapse: a security update that was never applied took down public healthcare IT systems for nine and a half hours on 13 October. Around 4,000 outpatient appointments were rescheduled, and some patients waited up to two hours.
