9to5Mac

The new Apple TV doubles its memory, but apps still can't use it

The US$299 Apple TV 4K has an A19 chip, 128GB of storage and 8GB of RAM, twice the previous model. But tvOS still caps each app at 2GB and force-quits any that go over, which 9to5Mac confirmed in its own tests. That matters most for demanding games, and it is unclear whether the limit is deliberate.

Next MacBook Neo expected to get an A19 Pro chip and 12GB of RAM

9to5Mac rounds up what is known about the follow-up to Apple's cheapest laptop. Bloomberg reports it will move from the A18 Pro to the A19 Pro, which benchmarks show is 10 to 15 per cent faster on the processor and up to 40 per cent on graphics, while memory should rise from 8GB to 12GB.

MacRumors

Apple's first smart glasses will lean on Siri, with no display

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman says Apple plans to unveil the glasses, code-named N50, at WWDC in June 2027 and ship them by the end of that year. Prototypes are slightly thinner than Meta's Ray-Bans and show no AR imagery. Siri uses the cameras to answer questions, while paired iPhone apps handle tasks like booking tables or rides.

Gurman says Apple wants four things for future iPhone Duo models: Face ID, an Action button, a telephoto camera and a thinner, lighter body. Prototypes of the second model already have Face ID on the outer display, but Apple is running out of time to finish it, so it could slip to the iPhone Duo 3.

Straits Times and Mothership

PM Wong asks ASEAN for a shared platform on companies behind haze fires

At the ASEAN Summit retreat in Manila, Lawrence Wong proposed a regional platform to coordinate and share information on companies whose fires cause cross-border haze, building on an existing Singapore-Indonesia task force. He also named Thailand-Cambodia border tensions and Myanmar as flashpoints the bloc must handle without breaking its consensus.

Straits Times

Simba's deadline to answer IMDA over spectrum use falls today

Singapore's fourth telco must explain by 16 November why it used radio frequencies it had not been assigned, at about 133 sites in 2022 and 2023. IMDA alleges senior management approved this. Simba could be fined or lose its licence, which would affect more than 1.4 million mobile subscribers. The probe already derailed its S$1.4 billion M1 deal.

Straits Times

Churches that are national monuments can now get 60-year leases

St Joseph's Church in Victoria Street, restored for about S$25 million, has had its lease renewed for 60 years, double the usual term for religious sites. A recent policy change lets gazetted places of worship get longer tenures to support upkeep, and at least two other monuments have been offered the same deal.

Straits Times

A cinema returns to Chinatown after a decade

Oriental Plaza on New Bridge Road, on the site of the old Oriental Theatre, will reopen as Shaw Oriental with Chinatown's only cineplex once refurbishment ends in early 2027. The area has had no cinema since 2016. A short walk away, the Lucky Chinatown mall by the MRT station may make way for a hotel.

Straits Times

Singapore-registered bulk carrier hits a bridge in New Jersey

The Strategic Alliance struck the Tacony-Palmyra Bridge early on 13 November, Singapore time. The Maritime and Port Authority says nobody was hurt, the ship was not badly damaged and there was no pollution. It is now moored in Pennsylvania, and MPA is investigating with US authorities. The bridge walkway is still shut for repairs.

Five stories from the day

Lalit Modi becomes a British citizen, complicating extradition

The IPL founder, who left India in 2010 and faces money-laundering and financial irregularity cases, revealed at the Oxford Union that he took UK citizenship in September. The India-UK treaty still allows extradition of British nationals, but officials say his new legal protections make returning him far harder in practice.

Indian Express

Government health spending has tripled in a decade

National Health Accounts figures show Union government health spending rose from ₹1.30 lakh crore in 2013-14 to ₹4.14 lakh crore in 2023-24. Over the same period, the share paid out of pocket by households fell from 64.2 to 42.7 per cent of total health spending, which the ministry credits to wider free and subsidised care.

Rajasthan police link a railway track blast to Pakistan's ISI

The state's anti-terror squad arrested Mahakdeep Kaur, 21, and Davinder Singh, 23, in Punjab's Gurdaspur over the 3 November blast on a track in Sri Ganganagar near the Pakistan border. Police say Kaur was in contact with two Pakistani handlers and that the pair had tried similar attacks before.

The Hindu

Cyclone Akvan expected to strengthen and head for Andhra Pradesh

The weather department says a depression over the Bay of Bengal should become a cyclonic storm today and a severe one by 17 November. Five stories from the day

Iran fires drones at a US base in Bahrain for the second time in three days

The Revolutionary Guards said six drones targeted a US air defence radar at the Al-Rifaa base in retaliation for American strikes, and Bahrain says all were intercepted. Iran also claims a mine sank an oil supertanker in the Strait of Hormuz, further dimming hopes for talks with Washington.

The Guardian

Estonia will close its whole border with Russia from 1 December

Eight people were detained on Saturday over an attempted arson and bomb attack on Threod Systems, which makes military drones. Estonia's security service says Russia's GRU ordered it. Prime Minister Kristen Michal said the closure is meant to prevent further attacks and tied the sabotage to Estonia's support for Ukraine.

The Guardian

A single router caused the US nationwide flight stoppage

Transport Secretary Sean Duffy says Friday's four-hour ground stop, the longest since 9/11, began when contractor L3Harris installed a router while swapping old copper lines for fibre. It flooded the air traffic network with connection attempts, and 17 of 22 control centres lost stable voice links. Flights are still disrupted.

BBC News

More than 350,000 rally in Prague to protect public broadcasters

Organisers say Sunday's protest on Letná Plain was one of the largest in Czech history. Crowds oppose Andrej Babiš's plan to scrap licence fees and fund Czech TV and Czech Radio from the state budget, which critics say would give ministers leverage over newsrooms, as happened in Hungary and Slovakia.

The Guardian

Four Western allies press Israel to let 150,000 tents into Gaza

Australia, Canada, Italy and the UK said in a joint statement that tents and other supplies are held at the border while recent floods swept away tens of thousands of shelters. They urged immediate, unimpeded access, saying there is still time to act before winter sets in. More than 1.9 million Gazans are displaced.

Models and products

China's first fully AI-made feature film flops at the box office

Sanxingdui: Future Past, made with no human actors or camera crew, has taken only about 1.8 million yuan (US$270,000) after three weeks in Chinese cinemas, according to box office tracker Beacon Pro. It is an early sign that audiences are not yet keen to pay for films generated entirely by AI.

South China Morning Post

Chinese labs start stripping hacking skills from open models

After several high-profile AI hacking incidents involving US and Chinese firms, China's leading model makers are limiting the cyber capabilities of their most advanced open-weight releases. Tencent says it removed hacking abilities from its latest Hy4 model while keeping its other strengths, raising the question of how much such limits cost in competitiveness.

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told Fox News that Nvidia needs a dramatic shake-up and its board should hire from outside the company to replace Jensen Huang. It asks the board to bring Huang back while an independent investigation runs, arguing he was pushed out without due process or evidence.

Tech in Asia

South Korea vows a crackdown after AI-assisted exam cheating

Han Won-seok, who topped the certified tax accountant exam with one wrong answer out of 160, admitted using a hidden camera to send questions to a generative AI tool and reading answers on his phone. The government held an emergency meeting, and anyone caught will lose the qualification and be barred from retaking it.
