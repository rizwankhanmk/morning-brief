From Apple

Apple opened the Apple Health AI Usage Study on 15 November, a US-only research app built with OpenEvidence that answers everyday health questions with cited clinical sources. Up to 200,000 adults aged 21 and over with an iPhone 15 Pro or later can join and optionally share HealthKit data. Apple says study data will not be used to train AI models.

Corey Fugman takes over Apple Pay and Wallet from Jennifer Bailey

Bloomberg reports that Fugman, a 26-year Apple veteran who joined in retail merchandising and later ran Apple Card and Apple Cash, will lead the payments and wallet unit. Bailey, who had run Apple Pay since its 2014 launch, was due to retire in October. The unit handles tens of billions of transactions a year in over 90 markets.

Server-backed features such as complex Siri AI requests, Image Playground and Clean Up now have daily allowances per device rather than per account. Apple is not publishing the numbers, which vary with demand. Basic requests like timers keep working once you hit the cap, and paying for a bigger iCloud+ plan raises it.

More families are returning to ComCare help within three years

About 54 per cent of households that left short-to-medium-term ComCare aid in 2022 came back within three years, up from 49 per cent for the 2021 group but below 63 per cent in 2019. MSF says patterns are returning to pre-pandemic norms; stable jobs kept families off aid, while caregiving and health problems pulled them back.

Five stories from the day

Fewer Indians are studying in the US for the first time in four years

The Open Doors report counts 339,435 Indian students at American universities in 2025-26, down from a peak of 363,019 the year before, a fall of about 6.5 per cent. India still sends more students to the US than any other country, but the drop ends a run of growth that began after the pandemic dip.

The filing became public on Monday; the RBI did not reply to questions.

Ukraine strikes the Crimea bridge in Russia's biggest reported drone barrage

Russia says it downed 2,049 drones and four long-range missiles in 24 hours, about 600 of them aimed at Moscow, and the Kerch bridge took minor damage that halted traffic. Kyiv faced a record 17 air raid alerts, with at least seven killed by Russian strikes, days after US, Ukrainian and Russian envoys held talks.

A royal decree put General Fayyad al-Ruwaili in charge of the coalition fighting the Houthis, a day after its forces pulled back from near the Bab al-Mandeb strait to defend Aden. The strait matters more than usual because Saudi oil has been routed that way while the Iran war chokes Hormuz.

The Verge

India signs a US-led declaration to protect the AI supply chain

The San Francisco Declaration, agreed by members of the Pax Silica initiative, treats the minerals, chips, factories, energy networks and logistics behind AI as shared assets to defend against economic coercion and market-distorting practices. India joined Pax Silica in February, and the move is widely read as a response to rivalry with China.
